Vectrex, online.

Plug your Vectrex into your computer and play against other Vectrex gamers over the internet. Make an account and your scores follow you.

You need a real Vectrex with a UVMC2 multicart (the cartridge with the USB port) to play these games: they run on the console itself, and the cartridge's USB link is what connects it to the internet.

Scores are kept in memory only on this server: they vanish when it restarts.
