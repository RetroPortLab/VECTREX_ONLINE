What you need

  1. A Vectrex with the UVMC2 cartridge, running VEC STRIKE.
  2. A USB cable from the cartridge to this computer.
  3. Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop. When you press Connect, pick the cartridge in the list.
  4. The cartridge shows up as two ports. If it doesn't answer, press Choose port… and pick the other one.
  5. Keep this tab open while you play. Close VectrexBridge and the IDE's debugger first: only one program can hold the cart.
