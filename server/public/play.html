Play

Plug your Vectrex console into this computer and play over the internet, right from this page. No app to install.

You need a Vectrex with a UVMC2 cartridge (the multicart with the USB port) plugged into this computer. The games run on the console itself.

Games on the cartridge

Send the latest games from this site to the cartridge's SD card over USB. The cartridge must be running a NetLink game (built with the USB monitor).

What you need

  1. A Vectrex with the UVMC2 cartridge, running VEC STRIKE, LIGHT CYCLES, CO-OP ASTEROIDS or the Link Test, built with the USB monitor on.
  2. A USB cable from the cartridge to this computer.
  3. Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop. When you press Connect, pick the cartridge in the list.
  4. The cartridge shows up as two ports. If it doesn't answer, press Choose port… and pick the other one.
